Methodology - Lead Scoring ABCD

How to use this dashboard:

Required file: leadscoring.csv
Export lead data with the AI score from your system.

  • Upload a CSV file containing leads with the following columns:
    • Required:
      • deal_created_at or created_at - Lead creation date
      • ia_score or score - Lead score (0-100)
    • Optional:
      • deal_status - Status (WON, LOST, OPEN)
      • c_tags or contact_tags - Contact tags
      • origin_name or origin_group_name - Lead origin
      • deal_id or contact_id - Unique identifier
      • segment or segmento - Market segment

ABCD classification:

  • A - Hot: Score ≥ 80 (hot leads, top priority)
  • B - Warm: Score 60-79 (warm leads, medium priority)
  • C - Cold: Score 40-59 (cold leads, low priority)
  • D - Low: Score < 40 (very cold leads)
  • Thresholds can be adjusted automatically by quantiles (P75, P50, P25)

Automatic processing:

  • Score normalization: Converts decimal values (e.g. 0.85 → 85)
  • Status normalization: Groups variations (won/ganho/ganha → WON)
  • Date parsing: Supports Brazilian and ISO formats
  • Column detection: Recognizes aliases automatically
  • Data validation: Diagnostics for null/invalid values
  • Global date filter: Analyze specific periods

Available visualizations:

  • Band → status sunburst: WON / OPEN / LOST mix within each band in a single ring
  • Band profile (parallel coordinates): volume, conversion, average score, % with tags and % paid origin
  • Band flow over time (stream): monthly volume per band as a river
  • Tags × bands heatmap: the 12 most frequent tags crossed with the bands
  • Top origins: the channels that feed each band the most
  • Leads per day and band: heatmap or lines, with outlier trimming
  • Conversion vs volume matrix: each band positioned by volume and conversion rate, with quadrants
  • Monthly calendar: daily volume with the band mix in the tooltip
  • Summary tables: numeric breakdown for verification

Usage tips:

  • Click bars, bubbles or cells in the charts to apply cross filters; active filters appear as chips in the top bar
  • Use the global period in the top bar to slice the whole analysis
  • The A/B/C/D bands use an ordinal blue ramp: the darker, the better the band; green and red are reserved for status (WON/LOST)

Important notes:

  • 100% safe: No data is sent to servers (local processing)
  • Performance: Optimized for datasets with up to 50,000 records
  • Leads without a score: fall into band D and show up in the diagnostics below
