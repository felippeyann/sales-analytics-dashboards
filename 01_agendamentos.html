Methodology - Bookings Analysis

What this panel answers

  • Where the top of the funnel wins and loses, from origin to booking: by squad, channel, campaign, SDR, closer, score and speed (TA).

Input data

  • Single file agendamentos.csv: a CRM activity export, 1 row per activity. The full contract (columns, types and template) is in the "How to build the CSV" panel of the data bar at the top of the page.
  • Only rows with title = "Agendamento de reunião" and type = SCHEDULE enter the analysis; the rest of the export is ignored.
  • Parsing happens 100% in the browser: no data leaves your machine.

Definitions and derivations

  • Score: lead_score becomes an ordinal band: A ≥ 80, B ≥ 60, C ≥ 40, D below 40. In the charts the band uses a single-hue ramp (darker = better).
  • TA (time to booking): days between deal_created_at and ta_created_at, grouped into 7 bands (up to 1.5 days through over 30).
  • Corporate vs free email: based on the contact's email domain (list of known free providers).
  • Normalizations: UTM sources (fb/meta become Facebook Ads etc.) and closer names (aliases and spelling variations).

Interaction

  • Cross-filter: click any node, slice, cell or block to filter the whole panel by that dimension; active filters become chips in the top bar and combine with each other (logical AND).
  • Period: the date filter uses the booking activity date (ta_created_at).
  • Per chart: analyze with AI (Gemini, with your own key), download a PNG, export the aggregated data as CSV.
  • Export PDF generates a report of the whole page.
