Methodology · NewsRadar Editorial

How to use this dashboard:

Required file: newsradar_artigos.csv
Editorial monitoring export, one row per captured article.

  • Upload a CSV file containing articles with the following columns:
    • Required:
      • título or title or headline: article title
      • data or date: publication date
      • fonte or source: source/outlet
    • Optional:
      • score: relevance score (0-100)
      • url or link: article link
      • tags: comma-separated keywords
      • categoria: editorial category

Automatic processing:

  • Smart tokenization: removes Portuguese stopwords and normalizes terms
  • Keyword detection: extraction from title + tags
  • Co-occurrence analysis: relationships between keywords
  • Time aggregation: grouped by week
  • Date parsing: supports DD/MM/YYYY, YYYY-MM-DD and ISO
  • Deduplication: by URL (or title + date), keeping the first occurrence

Available visualizations:

  • Theme river: weekly stream of the 6 strongest keywords (band thickness = mentions)
  • Top keywords: treemap of the 20 most mentioned keywords plus an aggregated "Other" block
  • Score distribution by source: one dot per scored article, with each source's average marked as a diamond (top 8 sources by volume)
  • Keyword Explorer: word cloud, co-occurrence network (up to 30 nodes) and weekly heatmap (up to 30 rows)
  • Theme flow by source: Sankey with the top 5 sources and top 5 keywords
  • Top articles: full listing with filters and sorting

Available filters:

  • Time window: last 30, 60, 90 days or the entire period
  • Source: filter by a specific outlet
  • Keyword: click any keyword chart to apply the cross-filter
  • Keyword search: text field that refines the rankings
  • Table filters: search by title, tags and minimum score

Usage tips:

  • Use the blue chips in the filter bar to see and remove active slices
  • Click blocks, bands, dots and nodes to apply the keyword or source cross-filter
  • Export the cleaned dataset via "Export data (.csv)"
  • Each card has an actions menu with Download PNG and Export CSV

Technical notes:

  • 100% local: CSV parsing happens in the browser; no data ever leaves your machine
  • Performance: optimized for up to 10,000 articles
  • Automatic cleanup: removes records without a title or date
  • Timezone: America/Sao_Paulo (UTC-3)
